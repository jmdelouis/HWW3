## Design notes

- [Algorithm](algorithm.md) of the grid generator: required cell size from

  adjacency, triangle quality, the explicit time step.
- [Numerics of the GPU model](wave_numerics.md): geometry and boundary
  flags, the explicit N scheme and its sub-cycling, the transpose and
  automatic differentiation, the time step, the layouts on the device.
- [Differentiable WW3](differentiable.md): the design, cost and plan of
  the differentiable model, in the section Learning the parameters.
